About This Tome

Let's get serious for a second:

I know a lot of you aren't actually wizards. I mean, I am, but a lot of you reading this are probably ordinary humans, and that's alright. I want this website to be for you non-magic folk, too.

Wizards and witches aren't the only ones who can benefit from having a grimoire. Let's say you're having a bunch of friends over, or a thirsty murder of crows. Maybe you want some refreshments for the table? Or you're running a DnD campaign, set in what you've convinced your players is a "fictional" fantasy land. Maybe you want some melodies to go along with the adventure? While this guide may be meant for magicians, anyone and everyone can look into its pages and find something that may just serve them well when it comes to the fantastical (or to emulating it).

My name is Nat, and I'm a 100% verified wizard and college student in a 'developing online content' class. All information and media found in this grimoire is either created by me, or properly credited at the appropriate section. (And if you're the kind of person who cares, this website was made using no AI or AI assistance.)

Thank you for clicking through! May the magic pages ahead open themselves up to you.
