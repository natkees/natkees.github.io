Melodies of Note

For your reference — some magical and fantastical beats, for adventuring, spellcasting, ritual studying, and other merry making activites:

  1. A classic! If you don't have a tower to call your own, manifest your own audiority copy with this "exploring a tower" melody. This is great for crystal ball scrying, studying for magical exams, and writing long papers on grimoires or other scrolls.

  2. Ah! My alma mater (before pursuing my earthly bachelor's, anyway). The mystical tunes in the aptly named "Wizard City" were like no other for spell casting and even the training of a familiar! Even the most stubborn toad or black cat could not resist these quaint notes of students practicing sigils on the common grounds, despite the undead running amuck!

  3. One of my favorite artists, inekt makes music for all moods extraordinary and every day. For even the smallest fetch-quest adventure, one of this artist's tunes ought to inspire your magic to its most agreeable. I liked one of my favorites, but more can be found exploring the Youtube channel.
